Agent routing

Every question finds its agent.

PICO sends each question to the right agent and shows the result in the right form.

  1. Your question arrives by voice or text.
  2. Router reads it and picks an agent.
  3. Agent works: Writer, Coder, Researcher or Designer.
  4. Result lands as an answer, canvas or live preview.
