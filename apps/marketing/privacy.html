What we store

  • Email: if you sign up or sign in.
  • Name: if you set one in your profile.
  • Plan: your current plan (Starter, Weekly Pass, or PICO Pro).
  • Referral: your referral code and rewards.
  • Usage counts: voice minutes used and session counts.

Why we store it

To sign you in, keep your chats, enforce plan limits, and calculate referral rewards. We do not track you without opt-in consent.

Analytics and crash reports

We collect automated, non-intrusive crash reports to fix bugs. Feedback is opt-in only.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Service providers, such as AI model and payment providers, receive only what they need to deliver the feature you use.

Your choices

You can delete your account from your profile. Deleting an account removes your user record, sessions, and linked data.
