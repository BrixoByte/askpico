Referral

Invite friends, earn voice time

Rewards are added after real activity, not just a sign-up.

How the link works

Sign in to get your personal code. Share your link, which looks like https://askpico.app/?ref=YOUR_CODE. When someone opens it, the code fills in automatically. Signing up without a code is also fine.

When rewards are given

  • Signing up alone gives no reward.
  • A reward is added after the referred user completes a real activity, such as their first voice session or email verification.
  • Rewards add minutes to your daily voice limit.

Anti-abuse rules

Using your own link, creating repeat accounts, or referring the same person again gives no reward. Every reward event is recorded, and we may review accounts that look fake.

Questions

For referral questions, write to hello@askpico.app.
