Features

What PICO does in the beta

Five core features are included in the beta. Advanced agent tools are coming after launch.

Voice mode

Speak your question and hear the answer. Voice works on Chrome and Edge, and a text fallback is always available. Daily voice time depends on your plan.

Smart model routing

PICO picks the right speed for each question. Choose Auto, or pick Quick, Smart, or Deep. You never need to choose a model yourself.

Agents

Ask in plain language and PICO sends your message to the right agent, such as Study or Business, each with its own style.

Artifacts and preview

Code and small web pages appear in a live preview next to the chat, so you can see the result as you go.

Persona and character

Each agent has a mood that changes with the conversation. The chibi character reacts to it, and motion stops when reduced motion is on.

Guest access

Try PICO without an account. Sign in with Google to keep your chats across devices.
